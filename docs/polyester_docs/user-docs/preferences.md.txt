# Overview
Open Settings to update how Polyester looks and displays information. Choices save in cookies on this browser.
Preferences change how Polyester looks, sounds, and displays information in this browser. They do not change your balances, orders, or account security.
## Open Settings
Click the gear in the top nav to open **Settings**. On the trading page, the same settings are also in the footer.
Update a control under **Personalization** or **Localization**. The change applies as you choose it.
![Settings menu opened from the gear in the top nav](https://media.polyester.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=800,quality=80,format=auto,fit=scale-down/content-assets/a/95871696978321d4330794194432457b1d66b94814b6da3fc954c96e7ef864de/source.webp)
## Where choices are saved
Your choices save in cookies on this browser only. They are not stored on your Polyester account. Another browser, another device, or cleared cookies starts from the defaults.
## Available settings
- [**Appearance**](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/appearance): theme, color tone, trading colors, and typeface
- [**Language & Region**](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/language-and-region): language, dates, times, currency, and numbers
- [**Sounds**](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/sounds): master volume and category levels
- [**Notifications**](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/notifications): which updates you receive and where they appear
- [**Balances**](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/balances): hide account amounts on screen
- [**Footer**](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/footer): the market ticker at the bottom of the trading screen
