What is Polyester?

Polyester is a decentralized exchange built on
Polyester Chain
route
Decentralized finance (DeFi) has had this problem for years. The same Asset is split across blockchains, exchanges, and lending markets that can't share liquidity. Polyester combines those markets, so the chain you start on doesn't limit what you can do next.
Core features
Permissionless and non-custodial
Polyester does not require KYC checks. Your
owner wallet
stays in control of your account and private keys.
Advanced spot trading
Dedicated order books and a spot interface give you execution tools, market data, and order controls.
High-performance matching
PolyEngine
matches up to 1 million transactions per second, with 1 ms 99th percentile (P99) latency.
Broad chain support

16

28

Unified Assets
Compatible versions of the same Asset from multiple chains become one Unified Asset. You can trade, lend, or borrow that Asset in one interface.
Capital-efficient lending and borrowing
Use supported Assets from any connected chain as collateral. Those Assets can keep working while you borrow.
Cross-account collateral and permissions
Assign members, roles, and application programming interface (API) keys across a
Main Account
and its
Subaccounts
Customizable workspace
You can arrange charts, order books, order entry, and account data around the markets you use.
Institutional APIs
Institutions can integrate through Financial Information eXchange (FIX), WebSocket, and Simple Binary Encoding (SBE). Other APIs are also available.
Developer and AI integrations
Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and software development kits (SDKs) connect developers and AI agents to Polyester markets. Developers and agents can then build on that liquidity.
Together, these features make Polyester the ultimate liquidity hub across supported chains and Assets. Polyester brings together unified liquidity with a high-performance chain, advanced exchange infrastructure, and open integrations built for the next generation of DeFi.
Deposit and withdraw
Each Main Account and Subaccount has chain-specific deposit addresses.
Zipper
verifies the source-chain transaction and creates a 1:1-backed
Zipped Asset
on Polyester Chain. The
Funding Account
credits the matching Unified Asset.
Deposit Funds
Send a supported Asset from an external wallet.
A withdrawal uses an available Zipped Asset route. Zipper then returns the native Asset to the external address. What you can move depends on current listings, routes, fees, and confirmation requirements.
Withdraw Funds
Send a balance out to an external wallet.
Spot trading
Polyester spot trading lets you trade supported Asset Pairs through dedicated order books with institutional-grade latency.
Most decentralized exchanges begin with the Assets already available on one chain. Polyester is built to bring Assets from both EVM and non-EVM chains into the same exchange, including Assets that rarely meet in one native decentralized venue.
Spot trading overview
Order books, order types, and how execution works.
The
Unified Trading Account
brings deposited balances into dedicated spot order books. Equivalent versions of the same Asset share one trading balance, removing the need for a different exchange or balance for every source chain.
This creates a path for markets that are difficult to serve in a chain-specific DEX.
Every
Pair
remains its own market with its own order book. Polyester unifies access to liquidity, not unrelated Assets or prices.

For a same-Asset transfer, Polyester keeps the source route and its 1:1 backing, then uses available inventory on the destination route to fill the withdrawal. Shared Unified Asset balances let liquidity across routes serve that transfer. You do not need a separate pool for every pair of chains.
Availability depends on current listings, supported deposit and withdrawal routes, destination inventory, fees, minimums, confirmation requirements, and route health. A cross-Asset swap also needs an available Pair and enough order-book liquidity.
Quick Swap
Convert one Asset into another and deliver it to a chosen wallet.
Lend and borrow
Polyester gives lenders and borrowers access to liquidity gathered from every supported route for an Asset, rather than a single chain-specific pool.
Chain-specific lending platforms often divide one asset into separate markets. USDT on Ethereum may sit in a different pool from USDT on Solana, even though both represent the same economic asset. Each pool has its own supply, demand, utilization, and rates.
Polyester brings supported versions of the same asset into one Unified Asset balance before that balance reaches the lending market. A USDT deposit from Ethereum and a USDT deposit from Solana can therefore participate in the same USDT lending market rather than competing in isolated chain-specific pools.
Polyester's portfolio-aware risk engine takes this capital efficiency further. Eligible Assets in your Unified Trading Account continue to contribute collateral value while they are committed to open orders, so placing an Asset on the book does not make that capital unproductive.

If you lend, you gain access to demand gathered around the Asset instead of one route. If you borrow, you gain access to supply gathered from every supported route. For the market as a whole, liquidity can become deeper and more useful because it is no longer divided by chain.
Unified liquidity
Polyester brings liquidity from supported chains into one place, giving every market access to deeper, more useful liquidity.
Polyester turns chains into connected routes around a shared exchange rather than separate destinations with separate liquidity.
A better trading experience.
You can access more supported Assets in one set of spot markets.
A better bridging experience.
You can deposit the same Asset through one supported chain and withdraw it through another.
A better cross-chain swap experience.
You can deposit, trade, and withdraw through one path across otherwise disconnected ecosystems.
A better lending experience.
You can lend or borrow equivalent Assets through one market instead of multiple chain-specific pools.
Underneath that experience, Polyester keeps the system verifiable. Zipper preserves route-specific, 1:1 backing for Assets brought onto Polyester Chain. The Funding Account records unified on-chain balances. PolyEngine provides low-latency order matching while account balances, active orders, and trading strategies remain private.
Your non-custodial owner wallet controls the Polyester smart wallet associated with your Main Account and Subaccounts. Polyester does not hold your private keys or become the owner of your account.

Polyester is a decentralized spot and lending exchange built on Polyester Chain. It supports deposits from Bitcoin, XRP Ledger, Bitcoin Cash, and most other major blockchains. The platform combines sub-millisecond order-book trading, lending pools, cross-chain withdrawals, advanced APIs, and customizable workspaces in one unified exchange.

How does privacy work on Polyester?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
Your trading activity on Polyester’s matching engine, including your Unified Trading Account balances, orders, and trading history, is private. Funding Account balances, deposits, withdrawals, and on-chain transfers are publicly viewable on PolyesterScan. Public market data shows completed trades without exposing which Polyester account placed each order.
