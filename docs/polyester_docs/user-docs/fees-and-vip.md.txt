# Trading Fees
View spot rates, maker rebates, referral benefits, and the assets you can use to pay trading fees.
Polyester charges a trading fee whenever a spot order fills. Your fee rate depends on your **VIP tier** and whether the fill is classified as **maker** or **taker**. For eligible buy orders, you can also choose which asset pays the fee.
There is no fee to place or cancel an order that does not fill.

Fees are calculated from the value of each completed fill. If an order fills in several parts, the applicable fee is calculated separately for each fill.
> **Your VIP tier determines your rate**
>
> This table shows each tier's base rate. Symbol-specific adjustments, rewards, and referral discounts can lower your effective rate. Authenticated API clients can call `GET /v1/spot/fee-rates` for exact rates by symbol. See [VIP Tiers](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/fees-and-vip/vip-tiers) to learn how your tier is determined and how to progress to the next level.
## Maker and taker fees
| Role  | When it applies                                                                   |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maker | Your order rests on the order book before another order fills against it.         |
| Taker | Your order fills immediately against liquidity already resting on the order book. |
A limit order can be both: any quantity that fills immediately pays the taker rate, while quantity that rests and fills later pays the maker rate.
## Maker rebates
Polyester supports maker rebates: a negative maker fee credits your account when an eligible maker order fills. Exact maker rebate rates are coming soon and will be published here.
## Choose how your fee is paid
Most exchanges decide the fee asset for you. On Polyester, an eligible buy order lets you choose **Quote** or **Received** from the **Fee Source** selector in the Order Form. Your choice appears again when you review the order.
- **Quote** pays the fee with the quote asset you are spending. When buying BTC/USDT, the fee is paid in USDT.
- **Received** deducts the fee from the base asset you receive. When buying BTC/USDT, the fee is paid in BTC.
The percentage rate does not change. The fee is calculated in the selected asset:
| Fee source | Calculation                              | Effect on a buy order                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Quote      | Fill value in the quote asset × fee rate | You spend slightly more quote asset and receive the full base quantity.  |
| Received   | Base quantity received × fee rate        | You spend the entered quote amount and receive slightly less base asset. |
Let's say Bitcoin is currently trading at **$100,000** and you buy **$1,000 of BTC** at a **0.08%** fee rate.
- With **Quote**, the fee is **0.80 USDT**. You spend 1,000.80 USDT and receive 0.01 BTC.
- With **Received**, the fee is **0.000008 BTC**. You spend 1,000 USDT and receive 0.009992 BTC after the fee.
Sell-order fees are deducted from the quote asset received from the sale. For example, selling 0.01 BTC for 1,000 USDT at a 0.08% rate returns 999.20 USDT after the 0.80 USDT fee.
> **Fee Source is available on eligible buy orders**
>
> Quote is the default. The Received option appears only when the selected market and order support paying with the base asset you receive.
## Referral discounts and rewards
Referral codes affect positive trading fees in two different ways:
| Who receives the benefit | Benefit                                      | What it applies to                                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Referred trader          | 4% lower trading fees                        | Positive maker or taker fees on eligible fills         |
| Referrer                 | 15% of the referred user's paid trading fees | The fee remaining after the referred trader's discount |
The referral benefit is calculated from the trading fee, not the order's full value.
Let's say a referred trader completes a **$1,000** fill at a **0.08%** fee rate:
1. The standard trading fee is **0.80 USDT**.
2. The 4% referral discount saves the trader **0.032 USDT**.
3. The referred trader pays **0.768 USDT**.
4. The referrer earns 15% of that paid fee, or **0.1152 USDT**.
Referral discounts and rewards apply only when a positive trading fee is charged. They do not create a negative fee or maker rebate.
See [Referrals](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/referrals/overview) for referral eligibility, links, rewards, and account-level reporting.
## FAQ
### What is the difference between maker and taker?
A maker order rests in the order book and adds liquidity, typically achieved through limit orders. A taker order executes against liquidity in the book, typically consumed by market orders. [See trading fees.](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/fees-and-vip/trading-fees)
### What fees does Polyester charge?
Polyester charges fees for trading, deposits, and withdrawals. Trading fees occur when an order fills. Deposits and withdrawals have minimal fees that cover network gas costs and include a small service fee for moving assets into or out of the platform.
### What are the trading fees on Polyester?
Trading fees depend on your VIP tier and whether you are a maker or a taker. Fees apply to the amount of the order that fills. See [Trading Fees](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/fees-and-vip/trading-fees) in our user docs for the current rates.
