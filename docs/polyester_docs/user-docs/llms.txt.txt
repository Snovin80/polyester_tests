# Polyester Documentation
> Canonical product documentation for Polyester users and developers.
Polyester is a decentralized exchange that brings assets and liquidity from multiple networks into a unified trading environment. Use the pages below as the canonical entry points for product behavior, workflows, security boundaries, and integration guidance.
Published entries are canonical for their documented subject. Entries labeled **Draft** describe work in progress and may omit an approved schedule, address registry, report, destination, or released workflow. The live product remains authoritative for availability and transaction terms.
## Start Here
- [What is Polyester?](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/start/what-is-polyester): How Polyester unifies Assets from supported chains for trading, lending, bridging, and swaps.
- [Key Concepts](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/start/key-concepts): Learn how Polyester moves Assets across chains, keeps balances on-chain, and processes order-book trades.
- [Create Your Account](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/start/create-account): Choose how to sign in, create your non-custodial Main Account, and protect the wallet that controls it.
- [Secure Your Account](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/start/secure-account): Protect your owner credential, add MFA, save recovery codes, and configure withdrawal and transfer whitelists.
- [First Deposit & Trade](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/start/first-deposit-and-trade): Deposit an Asset, make it available in Trading, and place your first spot Order.

- [Order Book](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/order-book): Read current buy and sell prices, the spread, and visible liquidity for one spot market.
- [Market Trades](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/market-trades): Follow recent public executions for the active Pair and see where trading is taking place.
- [Order Form](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/order-form): Choose a side, order type, amount, price, and execution controls before submitting an order.
- [Open Orders](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/open-orders): Monitor working and partially filled orders, then act on their remaining quantity.
- [Order Triggers](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/order-triggers): Monitor conditional and staged instructions before and after they create executable orders.
- [Order History](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/order-history): Review filled, canceled, expired, and rejected order outcomes for the selected account.
- [Funds](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/widgets/funds): Compare Unified Trading and Funding balances for the two Assets in the active Pair.
- [Market Order](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/market-order): Attempt immediate execution against available Order Book liquidity within price protection.
- [Limit Order](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/limit-order): Buy at a chosen maximum price or sell at a chosen minimum price.
- [Time-in-Force & Post-Only](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/time-in-force-and-post-only): Control what happens to unfilled limit quantity and whether an order may remove liquidity immediately.
- [Iceberg](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/iceberg): Understand the Iceberg order concept and its current availability on Polyester.
- [TWAP](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/twap): Spread a target quantity across scheduled child orders while monitoring execution, timing, and completion risk.
- [Ladder](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/ladder): Distribute a target quantity across planned limit-price levels and monitor each child order independently.
- [Stop-Loss Market](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/stop-loss-market): Attempt market-style execution after a downside condition while accounting for gaps, protection, and partial fills.
- [Stop-Loss Limit](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/stop-loss-limit): Activate a limit child after a downside condition while keeping trigger price and execution boundary distinct.
- [Trailing Stop](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/order-types/trailing-stop): Follow a favorable price extreme and activate a child order after price reverses by a configured distance.
- [Manage Orders](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/manage-orders): Track active orders, understand fills, modify remaining quantity, and confirm how each order ends.
- [Quick Swap](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/trade/quick-swap): Convert one supported asset into another and deliver it to a chosen wallet with Quick Swap.
