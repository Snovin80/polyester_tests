# Language & Region
Choose how Polyester displays language, dates, times, currencies, and numbers.
Language and regional settings make Polyester easier to use wherever you are. They change how information is displayed without changing your assets, transactions, or account history.
Open **[Preferences](https://testnet.polyester.com/account/settings/preferences)** from the gear in the top nav to choose the formats that feel most familiar to you.
## Language
Choose the language used for supported buttons, labels, and messages. Asset names, trading pairs, wallet addresses, and transaction IDs stay the same.
