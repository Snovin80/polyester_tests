How your tier is calculated
Spot trading volume
Polyester adds the USD value of your settled spot fills across the rolling 30-day window. Open,
canceled, unfilled, and failed-settlement quantities do not count.
30-day settled spot volume = settled spot fill value over the rolling 30-day window
Maker and taker fills both count. Activity from the Main Account and every Subaccount beneath it is combined into the same volume total.
Account equity
Polyester measures the USD value of your eligible account equity across the Main Account and all
of its Subaccounts, then averages hourly values across the rolling 30-day window.
30-day average account equity = total hourly account equity values / 720
Funding and Trading balances count toward the same account-level equity total. Moving an asset between your own accounts does not create additional equity, and a large new deposit must remain in the account long enough to raise the rolling average.
Examples
Qualify through trading volume.
Your settled 30-day spot volume is $6 million, which qualifies
for VIP 4. Your 30-day average account equity is $200,000, which qualifies for VIP 2. Polyester
applies
VIP 4
because the volume path gives you the higher tier.
Qualify through account equity.
Your settled 30-day spot volume is $600,000, which qualifies
for VIP 2. Your 30-day average account equity is $1.2 million, which qualifies for VIP 5.
Polyester applies
VIP 5
even though your trading volume is below the VIP 5 requirement.
Use the higher tier without combining paths.
If your volume qualifies for VIP 5 and your
equity qualifies for VIP 4, your effective tier is
VIP 5
VIP 6.
When your tier changes
Polyester evaluates complete qualification data hourly. Your tier can move up or down as new
activity enters each rolling window and older activity leaves it. Account-equity promotions require
the full threshold, while an existing account-equity tier is retained down to 95% of its threshold
to avoid frequent changes near a boundary.
A new tier applies to future fills; it does not recalculate fees on trades that have already
settled. Authenticated API clients can call
GET /v1/vip/status
to inspect the current component
tiers, metrics, and next-tier thresholds.
GET /v1/vip/tiers
returns the active public catalog.
FAQ
How do I increase my VIP tier to qualify for lower trading fees?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
Polyester offers lower trading fees through its VIP tiers, which are based on your trading volume or average account equity. You only need to meet one requirement, and the higher qualifying tier applies to your Main Account and its Subaccounts. See
VIP Tiers
in the user docs for the current qualification requirements.
