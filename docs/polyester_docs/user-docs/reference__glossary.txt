This glossary defines Polyester terminology. Each entry links to the page where the concept is
covered in depth. For a broader map of how these systems fit together, see
Key Concepts
Term
Definition
Applies to
Term
Address Book Entry
Definition
A saved destination address and its label.
Applies to
Wallet, Withdrawals, Security
Term
API key
Definition
A revocable credential used to access Polyester programmatically.
Applies to
API, Security, Desktop
Term
Base Asset
Definition
The first asset in a trading pair and the asset being bought or sold.
Applies to
Trading, Markets
Term
Deposit Flow
Definition
The complete journey of an asset moving from an external chain into Polyester.
Applies to
Funds, Zipper, Scan
Term
EOA
Definition
A blockchain account controlled directly by a private key.
Applies to
Accounts, Security
Term
Finality
Definition
The point when a chain transaction is considered sufficiently permanent.
Applies to
Deposits, Withdrawals, Chains
Term
Funding Account
Definition
The account area where deposits arrive and assets remain available for transfers or withdrawals.
Applies to
Funds, Transfers, Deposits
Term
Gas abstraction
Definition
Polyester's system for sponsoring Polyester Chain gas on supported actions.
Applies to
Polyester Chain, Accounts
Term
GuardSigner
Definition
A security signer used to approve protected account actions.
Applies to
Accounts, Security
Term
Index Price
Definition
A volume-weighted reference price built from normalized Asset prices across leading centralized and decentralized exchanges.
Applies to
Trading, Funds, Lending
Term
Main Account
Definition
The main Polyester account that owns its Subaccounts.
Applies to
Accounts, Subaccounts
Term
Maker
Definition
An order that rests on the order book and provides liquidity.
Applies to
Trading, Fees, API
Term
Market Maker
Definition
A participant or strategy that quotes buy and sell orders to provide liquidity to a market.
Applies to
—
Term
MFA
Definition
An additional identity check used to protect sensitive account actions.
Applies to
Accounts, Security
Term
Owner wallet
Definition
The external or embedded wallet that authorizes actions for your Polyester Smart account.
Applies to
Accounts, Security
Term
$P
Definition
The native gas asset of Polyester Chain. Polyester sponsors gas for supported product actions.
Applies to
Polyester Chain, Gas
Term
PolyEngine
Definition
Polyester's high-performance system for order admission, matching, and settlement.
Applies to
Trading, Matching, Settlement
Term
Polyester Chain
Definition
Polyester's EVM-compatible blockchain for accounts, assets, and protocol activity.
Applies to
Polyester Chain, Accounts, Assets
Term
Polyester Scan
Definition
The public explorer for Polyester Chain activity, contracts, assets, and Zipper Flows.
Applies to
Scan, Explorer
Term
Post-only
Definition
An order setting that prevents the order from immediately executing as a taker.
Applies to
Trading, Orders, API
Term
Quote Asset
Definition
The second asset in a pair and the asset used to express its price.
Applies to
Trading, Markets
Term
Route
Definition
The configured connection between one asset on an external chain and its matching Zipped Asset on Polyester Chain.
Applies to
Zipper, Funds, Deposits
Term
Smart account
Definition
The Safe account on Polyester Chain that represents a Polyester account.
Applies to
Accounts, Polyester Chain
Term
Subaccount
Definition
A separate account under a Main Account with its own balances, members, and activity.
Applies to
Accounts, Trading, API
Term
Taker
Definition
An incoming order that executes against liquidity already on the order book.
Applies to
Trading, Fees, API
Term
Unified Asset
Definition
The account-facing balance that combines compatible source-chain asset routes.
Applies to
Funds, Trading, Zipper
Term
Unified Trading Account
Definition
The private balance area used for trading, lending, collateral, and margin.
Applies to
Trading, Funds, Margin
Term
Vault
Definition
A protected external custody location used by Zipper for a specific asset route.
Applies to
Zipper, Custody
Term
Withdrawal Flow
Definition
The complete journey of an asset moving from Polyester to an external chain.
Applies to
Funds, Zipper, Scan
Term
Zipped Asset
Definition
The Polyester Chain representation of one asset from one source chain.
Applies to
Funds, Zipper, Scan
Term
Zipper
Definition
Polyester's protocol for bringing supported external assets onto and off Polyester Chain.
Applies to
Funds, Deposits, Withdrawals
