# Footer
Choose what appears in the ticker at the bottom of the trading screen.
Footer settings control the market ticker along the bottom of the trading screen. Choose which markets it follows, how quickly it moves, and how much information it shows.
Open **Footer Settings** from the trading footer or use Quick Settings to show or hide it.
![Market ticker along the bottom of the trading screen](https://media.polyester.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=800,quality=80,format=auto,fit=scale-down/content-assets/a/a3ff41988fdf5f886ea3852fb72fa0c567440a352fbed8db0663e89f8dc63257/source.webp)
## Footer
Turn **Footer** on when you want the market ticker visible. Turn it off when you want more room for the trading workspace.

## Ticker category
Ticker category selects the group shown in the footer, such as Favorites, Popular, Gainers, Losers, or New listed.
Choose **Favorites** when you want the footer to follow the markets you selected yourself.
