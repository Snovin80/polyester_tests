Members & Roles
Members & Roles
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
Members let you share one
Subaccount
without sharing your entire Polyester account. Each person receives a role that applies only to that Subaccount.
Open the Subaccount and select
Members
to invite someone, update their role, or remove their access.
Invite a member
1
Choose Invite Member
Open the Subaccount's Members tab and select
Invite Member
2
Find the person
Search by username, wallet address, or account ID and select the correct Polyester account.
3
Choose a role
Give the person the role that matches what they need to do.
4
Send the invitation
Select
Invite member
Choose the right role
Role
Best for
Access
Owner
The person responsible for the Subaccount
Full account and access management
Admin
Someone who manages day-to-day account setup
Trading, transfers, members,
API keys
, and most settings
Treasury
Someone responsible for assets
Balances, internal transfers, and withdrawals
Trader
Someone responsible for trading
Balances and spot trading
Viewer
Someone who only needs visibility
Read-only account access
The Subaccount's permissions can narrow what any role is allowed to do. See
Permissions & Policies
for those account-wide controls.
Require MFA for members
The Owner can turn on
Require
MFA
for the Subaccount. Members who have not set up MFA are prompted to add it before using protected account actions.
This setting applies to members of the selected Subaccount. Each person manages their own MFA from their Polyester account.
Update or remove access
Change a member's role when their responsibilities change, or remove them when they no longer need the Subaccount. Removing a member ends their access but does not change completed trades or transfers.
