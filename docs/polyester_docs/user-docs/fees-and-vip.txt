Polyester charges a trading fee whenever a spot order fills. Your fee rate depends on your
VIP tier
and whether the fill is classified as
maker
or
taker
There is no fee to place or cancel an order that does not fill.

Maker and taker fees
Role
When it applies
Maker
Your order rests on the order book before another order fills against it.
Taker
Your order fills immediately against liquidity already resting on the order book.
A limit order can be both: any quantity that fills immediately pays the taker rate, while quantity that rests and fills later pays the maker rate.
Maker rebates
Polyester supports maker rebates: a negative maker fee credits your account when an eligible maker order fills. Exact maker rebate rates are coming soon and will be published here.
Choose how your fee is paid
Most exchanges decide the fee asset for you. On Polyester, an eligible buy order lets you choose
Quote
or
Received
from the
Fee Source
selector in the Order Form. Your choice appears again when you review the order.
Quote
pays the fee with the
quote asset
you are spending. When buying BTC/USDT, the fee is paid in USDT.
Received
deducts the fee from the
base asset
you receive. When buying BTC/USDT, the fee is paid in BTC.
The percentage rate does not change. The fee is calculated in the selected asset:
Fee source
Calculation
Effect on a buy order
Quote
Fill value in the quote asset × fee rate
You spend slightly more quote asset and receive the full base quantity.
Received
Base quantity received × fee rate
You spend the entered quote amount and receive slightly less base asset.
Let's say Bitcoin is currently trading at
$100,000
and you buy
$1,000 of BTC
at a
0.08%
fee rate.
With
Quote
, the fee is
0.80 USDT
With
Received
, the fee is
0.000008 BTC
Sell-order fees are deducted from the quote asset received from the sale. For example, selling 0.01 BTC for 1,000 USDT at a 0.08% rate returns 999.20 USDT after the 0.80 USDT fee.

Referral discounts and rewards
Referral codes affect positive trading fees in two different ways:
Who receives the benefit
Benefit
What it applies to
Referred trader
4% lower trading fees
Positive maker or taker fees on eligible fills
Referrer
15% of the referred user's paid trading fees
The fee remaining after the referred trader's discount
The referral benefit is calculated from the trading fee, not the order's full value.
Let's say a referred trader completes a
$1,000
fill at a
0.08%
fee rate:
The standard trading fee is
0.80 USDT
The 4% referral discount saves the trader
0.032 USDT
The referred trader pays
0.768 USDT
The referrer earns 15% of that paid fee, or
0.1152 USDT
Referral discounts and rewards apply only when a positive trading fee is charged. They do not
create a negative fee or maker rebate.
See
Referrals
for referral eligibility, links, rewards, and account-level reporting.
FAQ
What is the difference between maker and taker?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
A maker order rests in the order book and adds liquidity, typically achieved through limit orders. A taker order executes against liquidity in the book, typically consumed by market orders.
See trading fees.
What fees does Polyester charge?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
Polyester charges fees for trading, deposits, and withdrawals. Trading fees occur when an order fills. Deposits and withdrawals have minimal fees that cover network gas costs and include a small service fee for moving assets into or out of the platform.
What are the trading fees on Polyester?

Trading Fees
in our user docs for the current rates.
