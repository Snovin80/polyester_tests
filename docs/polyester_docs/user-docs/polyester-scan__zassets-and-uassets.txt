zAssets & uAssets
zAssets & uAssets
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
Polyester Scan
shows two connected asset types. A
Zipped Asset
, or
zAsset
, represents one asset from one source chain. A
Unified Asset
, or
uAsset
, brings compatible Zipped Assets together as one account-facing balance.
For example, USDT deposited from Ethereum and USDT deposited from Solana create separate Zipped Assets. When both
routes
map to Unified USDT, you see one USDT balance in your Polyester account.
Browse Unified Assets
The
Unified Assets directory
lists each Unified Asset with its supported chains, Unified Asset ID, Ledger ID, decimals, and combined Zipped Asset supply.
Expand a row to see every route behind that asset. Each route includes its source chain, Polyester contract, and source-chain contract. Native assets show
Native
instead of a source contract address.
On a Unified Asset page, you can use:
Flows
to see related deposits, withdrawals, and transfers
Zipped Assets
to compare the routes that contribute to the balance
Info
to see IDs, decimals, and route metadata
Holders
when holder information is available.
Browse a Zipped Asset
Open a Zipped Asset from the Tokens directory, a transfer, a Flow, or a Unified Asset page. Its detail page connects the route's source chain and source asset to the corresponding contract on
Polyester Chain
It can also show transfers, holders, flows, supply, and contract information. The supply shown here belongs to this individual source-chain route. A
Verified
badge means Scan recognizes the asset from Polyester's configured catalog.

USDT from Ethereum and USDT from Solana can use the same symbol while remaining separate Zipped Assets. Their source chains and contract addresses show which route each one represents.
Use
Asset Flows
to follow a specific movement, or
Tokens & Chains
to explore the routes available in Scan.
