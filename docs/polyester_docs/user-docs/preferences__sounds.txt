Sound settings control the audio feedback you hear while using Polyester. Turn everything on or off at once, then adjust each type of sound separately.
Open
Audio Settings
to choose your levels and preview the result.
Interactive
Enable audio
Turn
Enable audio
off when you want Polyester to stay silent. Your individual volume choices remain saved for the next time you turn audio on.
Set the overall volume
Master volume controls the overall level for every Polyester sound. Start here, then fine-tune the categories underneath it.
Adjust each category
Notifications
controls sounds for account and product alerts.
UI sounds
controls feedback from buttons and other interface actions.
Voice alerts
controls supported spoken updates.
Use the play button beside a category to hear its current level.
Choose a sound pack
Sound packs change the style of supported Polyester sounds without changing which alerts you receive. Choose a pack and use the preview before keeping it.
If nothing plays, check Enable audio, Master volume, the category level, your device output, and whether the browser tab is muted.
