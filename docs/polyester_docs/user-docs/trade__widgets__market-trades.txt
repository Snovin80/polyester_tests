Market Trades
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
Market Trades is the live tape for the active Pair.
Each row is a completed public execution, not a resting order or an unactivated trigger.
What the widget shows
Field
What it means
Price
Price where the trade completed
Amount
Executed amount in the selected base or
quote Asset
unit
Time
Time the public execution was reported
Color
Displayed buy or sell direction for the trade print
The widget can show Amount in the base or quote Asset. Its volume threshold can also emphasize larger trades without removing the underlying executions from the market record.
Read recent activity
Market Trades shows where price discovery is happening and whether recent activity is clustered around a level. Compare it with the
Polyester Chart
for history and the
Order Book
for liquidity that is resting now.
