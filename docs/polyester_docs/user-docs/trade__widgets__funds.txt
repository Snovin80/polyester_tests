Open in
The Funds widget keeps the balances relevant to the active Pair beside the market.
It shows the base and
quote Assets
in both Unified Trading and Funding for the selected
Main Account
or
Subaccount
What the widget shows
Balance area
What it means
Unified Trading
Balance held for trading. Open orders and other active work can reserve part of it.
Funding
On-chain balance available to withdraw, transfer to another account, or move into Trading.
The widget follows the active Pair, so it is a focused balance view rather than a complete portfolio. Changing the Pair changes which two Assets appear.
Read a Trading balance
Open a Unified Trading amount's balance detail to compare its three values.
Value
What it means
Total Balance
Posted Trading balance before active reservations are subtracted
Reserved Balance
Amount set aside for orders, triggers, or other pending spends
Available Balance
Amount that can support a new eligible order or transfer
An accepted order can reduce Available Balance before any fill occurs. A reservation is a hold, not proof that the Asset has been spent.
Trigger families do not all reserve at the same moment:
Trigger or strategy
Reservation timing
Standalone stop-loss or take-profit
Reserves capital when the trigger is created
Standalone trailing stop
Reserves capital when the trigger is created
Standalone TWAP
No upfront trigger hold; each slice reserves when admitted
Standalone LINEAR Ladder
No parent hold; all children are admitted immediately and reserve individually
Attached take-profit, stop-loss, or trailing risk
Starts with zero reserve and arms after the parent fills
Reserved Balance follows the lifecycle of each order or trigger rather than applying one timing rule to every strategy.
Add or move funds
Use 'Deposit' to open the deposit page with the relevant Asset selected. Use 'Transfer' to move an available balance between eligible Polyester balance areas or accounts.
The widget does not combine balances between accounts. Confirm the active Main Account or Subaccount before moving funds or placing an order. The
Trading Account
page explains the complete balance and reservation model.
