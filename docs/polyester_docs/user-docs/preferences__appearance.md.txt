# Appearance
Choose your theme, colors, and typeface.
Appearance settings let you make Polyester look the way you prefer. Choose a light or dark theme, adjust the overall color tone, select your trading colors, and change the typeface.
Open **[Personalization](https://testnet.polyester.com/account/settings/personalization)** from the in the top nav to make changes. Your preview updates as you choose each option.
## Choose a theme
Use **Light** for a bright interface, **Dark** for a darker workspace, or **System** to follow your device setting automatically.

## Choose your trading colors
Trading colors control how positive and negative price movement appears on charts, tables, and market values. Choose the palette that makes gains and losses easiest for you to recognize.

## Saving your appearance
Your choices save automatically in the current browser and apply across its Polyester tabs. Another browser or device can use different appearance settings.
