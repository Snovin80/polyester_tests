Admission
Admission
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
Admission
is the first stage of every spot order.
PolyEngine
reviews your instruction and account together before the order can enter the market.
If the order is accepted, PolyEngine gives it an Order ID and reserves the balance it may spend. If it is not accepted, the order does not begin working and Polyester shows the reason.
What Admission checks
PolyEngine checks:
that your active account can trade the selected pair
that the market and order type are available
that the price and amount follow the market's minimums and increments
that time in force,
post-only
, and self-trade prevention work with the order
that the order stays within its slippage limit
which asset will pay the trading fee
that your Trading Account has enough available balance.
The current market rules always apply. A price or amount copied from an older order may no longer fit the pair's current minimums or increments.
How your balance is protected
Admission reserves only the balance the order may need. A sell order reserves the
base asset
you want to sell. A buy order reserves the
quote asset
required for the purchase and any applicable fee protection.
The reservation is not a fee or a second charge. It simply prevents another order, transfer, or withdrawal from spending the same balance while your order is active.
As your order progresses:
completed fills use the amount required for the trade
an unfilled portion keeps the balance it still needs reserved
cancellation or completion releases any amount the order no longer needs.
See
Trading Account
for more about available and reserved balances.
Market orders still have slippage protection
A market order tries to trade immediately, but it does not accept any price. Each Pair has a default slippage limit, but the user can select a different limit. Polyester applies the selected limit when it processes the order. The order can fill completely, fill partly, or end without a fill when eligible liquidity is unavailable within that limit.
The selected market state also matters. A market can allow normal trading, accept only post-only orders, allow cancellations only, or stop accepting new orders.
What acceptance means
Acceptance means your order passed Admission and received an Order ID. The order can then move to
Matching
It does not guarantee that the order will fill. The result still depends on its price, time in force, available liquidity, and other order settings.
