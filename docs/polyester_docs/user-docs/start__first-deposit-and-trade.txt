First Deposit & Trade
First Deposit & Trade
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
Your first trade starts with an Asset you already hold. Deposit that Asset into Polyester, make the balance available in Trading, find a market that exchanges it for the Asset you want, and place an Order. After the Order fills, the Asset you received appears in your
Unified Trading Account
Before you deposit
Confirm these first:
Create an account
so Polyester can assign a deposit address
Secure your account
so the
owner wallet
and
MFA
are in place
Confirm the selected
Main Account
or
Subaccount
so the credited balance appears there
Hold a
supported Asset
on the network you'll send from
Deposit, trade, and check your new balance
This journey works whether your completed Deposit credits Funding first or goes directly to Unified Trading.
1
Deposit an Asset
Open
Deposit
, select the Asset, and choose the network where it currently exists. Copy the address directly from Polyester and send the Asset from your source wallet or exchange. Include the exact memo or destination tag when one is shown.

Each account gets one deposit address per supported chain, not per Asset. Supported EVM networks share that EVM address. The wrong network, address, or memo can prevent a Deposit from crediting. Verify before sending.
Wait for the required source-chain confirmations. If the completed Deposit appears in Funding, open
Transfer
and move the amount you want to trade into Unified Trading. If it already appears as an available Trading balance, continue to the next step.

Your Default Deposit Destination can be Funding or Unified Trading. You can review this setting under
Deposit Routing
on
Security
Deposit Funds
Learn more about depositing funds.
Internal Transfers
Learn more about internal transfers.
2
Find a market for the deposited Asset
Open
Spot Markets
and search for the ticker you deposited. Choose a Pair that contains both the Asset you have and the Asset you want to receive.

Base Asset
Quote Asset
BTC/USDT
, selling
BTC
gives you
USDT

BTC

Trading Screen
Learn more about the trading screen.
3
Choose Buy or Sell and place the Order
Choose
Sell
when you hold the Base Asset and want the Quote Asset. Choose
Buy
when you hold the Quote Asset and want the Base Asset. Confirm the active account, enter the amount, select a Market or Limit Order, review the estimated total and fees, then place the Order.

Market or Limit?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-3 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-3 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_ul]:my-3 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 text-secondary-foreground [&_strong]:text-foreground mt-0.5">
A
Market Order
prioritizes immediate execution within its protections. A
Limit Order
lets you control the worst execution price, but it may remain open until the market reaches that price.
Example: Buy BTC with a Limit Order
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
You hold
USDT
in Unified Trading and want
BTC
Open the
BTC/USDT
market.
USDT
is the Quote Asset you spend, and
BTC
is the Base Asset you want to receive.
Select
Buy
, choose a Limit Order, enter the amount of
BTC
, and set a Limit Price.
Review the Order and place it. When it fills, the spent
USDT
decreases and the received
BTC
appears in the same Unified Trading Account.
A filled Limit Buy shows Fill Progress at 100%, with no remaining quantity. Open the order to see the Limit Price, filled value, and the trades that completed it.
If a Limit Order remains open, the unfilled quantity stays reserved. Cancel the remaining Order if you want to release that balance back to Unified Trading.
Order Form
Learn more about the order form.
4
Check the Asset you received
After the Order fills, open your Unified Trading balance and confirm that the Asset you received is available.
Open Orders
shows any quantity still waiting to trade.
Order History
and
Fills
show what executed, including the quantity and price.

An accepted Order is not always a completed trade. If a Limit Order is still open or partially filled, some of its balance remains reserved until it fills or you cancel it.
The received Asset can support another eligible Order or be transferred back to Funding when it is available and not reserved.
Manage Orders
Learn more about managing orders.
Once the received Asset appears in your Unified Trading balance, you can use it in another eligible Order or transfer it to Funding for a supported withdrawal.
Withdraw Funds
Learn more about withdrawing funds.
FAQ
Where do I start, and what do I need to use the testnet?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
Start by creating a Polyester account with a supported wallet, Google, or email. If you want to claim paper assets, connect your Discord and X accounts to your Polyester account. You don’t need an existing crypto wallet if you sign up with Google or email. The
Start Here
section of the user docs guides you through protecting your account and making your first deposit and trade.
What features can I test now?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
You can trade spot markets, manage your orders, deposit, withdraw, and transfer. You can also customize your trading workspace with widgets, manage sub-accounts, configure account security, create
API keys
for trading tools, and track transactions on PolyesterScan.
