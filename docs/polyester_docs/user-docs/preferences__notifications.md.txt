# Notifications
Choose which Polyester updates you receive and how they appear.
Notifications keep you updated on account activity, trading, funds, security, and Polyester announcements. Choose the updates you care about and where you want to receive them.
Open **[Notifications](https://testnet.polyester.com/account/notifications)** to review alerts. Open **[Notification Settings](https://testnet.polyester.com/account/settings/notifications)** to manage your choices.
![Notifications with category filters and a selected alert](https://media.polyester.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=800,quality=80,format=auto,fit=scale-down/content-assets/a/3bb88241422aaf4e0b8202ff19d8755f687d94ac89b26629247e835407d2ac30/source.webp)
## Choose where notifications appear
- **In-app** notifications appear while you are using Polyester.
- **Browser** notifications can appear outside the active Polyester tab.
- **Push** notifications appear on supported devices.
Browser and push notifications also need permission from your browser or device.
## Choose which updates you receive
Turn notification groups on or off for:
- orders and trades
- deposits and withdrawals
- transfers
- security activity
- platform updates.
Your security settings and activity records remain available even when a notification group is turned off.
## Choose how alerts look and sound
Set where alerts appear on screen, whether Polyester shows an unread badge, how long temporary alerts remain visible, and whether they play a sound.
![Notification position options for on-screen alerts](https://media.polyester.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=800,quality=80,format=auto,fit=scale-down/content-assets/a/9a94cc380e0bef747935e5c71b9794c39fe8afcbb4f0fcc3f43b1f56d41cab15/source.webp)
Sound volume is controlled separately under [Sounds](https://testnet.polyester.com/docs/user-docs/preferences/sounds).
## If an alert does not arrive
Check that the notification group and delivery channel are on. For browser or push alerts, also check the browser permission, device notification settings, and focus or do-not-disturb mode.
