Trading Account

The
Unified Trading Account
is where you use Assets for spot trading and borrowing. It maintains private, low-latency
Unified Asset
balances for Orders, Fills, eligible collateral, and transfers.
Each
Main Account
and
Subaccount
has its own Trading Account, balances, Orders, and activity. Switching the active account changes what you see and what you can use, so check the selected account first if an expected balance or Order appears to be missing.
What the Trading Account does
Trading keeps the balances used by
PolyEngine
separate from the on-chain balances held in Funding.
Makes Assets available for trading.
Available balances can support new spot Orders on Polyester's order books.
Keeps Trading activity private.
Your balances, Orders, Fills, and strategies are visible only to your account.
Separates available and reserved amounts.
Trading shows what the account holds, what is free to use, and what is already committed.
Supports borrowing and margin.
Eligible Assets can contribute collateral value and support borrowing or other margin obligations. Holding an Asset in Trading does not lend it out or make it earn yield.
Keeps accounts separate.
Every Main Account and Subaccount has its own Trading balances and activity.
How Trading balances work
Three quantities describe each Unified Asset balance:
Total Balance
is the complete amount of the Asset held in Trading.
Available Balance
is the amount free for a new Order, borrowing action, transfer, or withdrawal.
Reserved Balance
is the amount already committed to an active Order, loan, withdrawal, transfer, or another pending action.
Polyester updates Available and Reserved Balance automatically as activity begins and ends. When an action commits an amount, that amount becomes Reserved. Any unused amount returns to Available when the action fills, is canceled, completes, or reaches another final state.

Read your balance
The Assets table shows the private Unified Asset balances held in the selected Trading Account. Total Balance shows the full amount held in Trading, while Available Balance shows what the account can use for a new action right now.
Open a Total Balance to see its Reserved Balance, or choose an available action when you are ready to trade or transfer the Asset.

Total, Available, and Equity answer different questions. Total tells you how much of an Asset Trading holds. Available tells you how much is free to use. Equity estimates the Total Balance's value using the Asset's Index Price, a volume-weighted reference built from normalized prices across leading centralized and decentralized exchanges.
Use Assets in Trading
An available Trading balance can support several actions:
Place a spot Order
Buy or sell an Asset through an available Pair and its order book.
Transfer to another Polyester account
Send an available balance to another eligible account.
Borrow against eligible collateral
Use an eligible Trading Asset as collateral when you borrow another Asset.
Withdraw an Asset
Send an available balance to an external address through an available destination
route
Only Available Balance can begin a new action. If part of the balance is Reserved, the existing Order or pending activity must release it before it can be used again.
Trading and Lending
Eligible Assets in Trading can contribute to account collateral and borrowing power. Polyester's risk engine can continue to recognize eligible collateral committed to open Orders, allowing the same capital to support trading and borrowing when account requirements are met.
Borrowing introduces liquidation risk. If the account no longer meets its margin requirements, eligible collateral in Trading may be sold to repay its obligations.

Holding an Asset in Trading does not create debt by itself. Liquidation risk begins when collateral supports borrowing or another margin obligation.
Transfer between Funding and Trading
Use
Transfer
when an available Funding balance should support trading, borrowing, or margin, or when an unused Trading balance should return to Funding.
1
Open Transfer
Keep
Between My Accounts
selected and review any values already shown in the form.
2
Choose the source and destination
Select Funding as the source and Unified Trading as the destination to move an Asset into Trading. Reverse them to return an available Trading balance to Funding. Use the same Main Account or Subaccount on both sides.
3
Choose the Asset and amount
Select an Asset held by the source account and enter an amount within its Available Balance.
4
Review and transfer
Confirm the account, direction, Asset, and amount, then select
Transfer
once. Wait for Transfer History and both balances to show the result before using the balance or trying again.
Use
Withdraw
when the destination is an external chain. A withdrawal from Trading reserves the amount, moves it through Funding and
Zipper
, and remains subject to the route's inventory, fees, minimums, policies, permissions, and security controls.

Funding and Trading can show the same Unified Asset, but they serve different purposes. Funding is the on-chain balance area for deposits, transfers, and withdrawals. Trading is the private, low-latency balance area used by Orders, reservations, Fills, fees, and eligible collateral for borrowing.

Moving an Asset into Trading does not bridge it, change its owner, create another
Zipped Asset
, or move the underlying Zipped Asset out of Funding. It changes which part of the same Polyester account can use the corresponding Unified Asset balance.
Funding-to-Trading transfers settle as one Polyester Chain transaction and normally appear in Trading almost immediately. Once the transfer completes, the updated Trading balance is ready to use.
