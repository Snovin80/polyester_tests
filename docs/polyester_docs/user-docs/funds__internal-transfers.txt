Internal Transfers
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
Internal transfers move an available balance without leaving Polyester. You can move assets between Funding and Unified Trading, between your
Main Account
and
Subaccounts
, or to another Polyester user.
Because both sides are Polyester accounts, an internal transfer does not use an external chain, deposit address, or
Zipper
route
fee. Open the
Transfer page
to get started.
Make an internal transfer
Open
Transfer
from the account that holds the balance. Choose a tab, then set the source, destination, Asset, and amount.
Between My Accounts
To Another Polyester User
Use this when both sides belong to you.
1
Choose From
Select the sending account and Funding or Unified Trading. The form shows the available balance for that source.
Transferring 541.64 USDT from Main Funding to Futures Funding
2
Choose To
Select your Main Account or a Subaccount as the destination. Then choose Funding or Unified Trading for that account. From and To each have their own account and balance area. Use the swap control if you want to reverse the direction.
3
Choose the Asset and amount
Select the Asset, then enter the amount, use
Max
, or move the percentage slider. Stay within the available balance shown under the slider.
4
Review and confirm
Compare From, To, Asset, and amount. Then approve the request in the connected wallet.
Using the wallet to confirm the internal transfer
5
Confirm the completed transfer
When Ledger Settlement Confirmed completes, check the transferred amount and both balances.

What happens after you submit
Submitting the request begins the Transfer Flow. Polyester then moves it through two stages:
Interactive
1
Transfer Observed
2m ago
2
Ledger Settlement Confirmed
45s ago
Transfer Flow
1
Transfer Observed
Polyester records the internal movement between the selected accounts and balance areas.
2
Ledger Settlement Confirmed
The Unified Asset leaves the source Available Balance and appears at the destination. There is no Zipper route fee.
You can see more details on
Polyester Scan

Funding and Unified Trading transfer routes
Every side of an internal transfer identifies both a Polyester account and a balance area. Moving between Funding and Unified Trading changes where the balance can be used. Moving between a Main Account and Subaccount changes which account owns it.
From
To
Result
Funding
Funding
Moves an available Funding balance to the destination account's Funding balance
Funding
Unified Trading
Makes an available Funding balance available in the destination account's Trading balance
Unified Trading
Funding
Moves an available, unreserved Trading balance to the destination account's Funding balance
Unified Trading
Unified Trading
Moves an available Trading balance to the destination account's Trading balance
Funding transfers use the available Funding balance. Unified Trading transfers use
Available Balance
, so open orders and other pending actions can reduce the amount you can move.
Track the transfer
Transfer submitted
means Polyester accepted the request. Transfer History is the primary record and shows the time, amount, asset, source, destination, Flow ID, and current status.
Viewing Transfer History after a 541.64 USDT internal transfer
Follow the same history record until it reaches a final state and both balances reflect the movement. Do not submit a second transfer while the first result is still unclear.
Some internal transfers include public
Polyester Chain
evidence, while others are private balance movements. A missing public Scan record does not mean the internal transfer did not occur; use Transfer History as the account record.
Internal Address Book and Transfer Whitelist
An
Address Book
entry saves another Polyester account so it is easier to find again. Saving a recipient does not automatically authorize transfers to that account.
If the source account requires the
Internal Transfer Whitelist
, the recipient must also have an active whitelist record for that source. Each Main Account and Subaccount has its own saved entries and transfer controls.
FAQ
Can I transfer to a disabled Subaccount?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
No. A disabled Subaccount rejects new inbound activity until it is active again.
Learn about internal transfers.
What is the difference between the transfer options?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
‘Between My Accounts’ moves funds between your Main Accounts, your Subaccounts, and Subaccounts you are a member of, where your permissions allow. ‘To Another Polyester User’ sends funds to a Polyester account resolved from a username, account ID, or
smart account
address.
Do I need to accept an incoming internal transfer?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
No. If you are expecting an incoming internal transfer, the funds will arrive in your
Funding Account
or
Unified Trading Account
without any further action from you.
Learn about internal transfers.
What does the recipient of an internal transfer see about the sender?

The recipient's Ledger can show the sending Polyester Account ID or Subaccount ID, its public smart-account address, and the sender's configured username or account label.
