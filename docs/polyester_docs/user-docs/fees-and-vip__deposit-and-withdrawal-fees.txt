Polyester charges deposit and withdrawal fees
in the asset being deposited or withdrawn
native gas asset
for Polyester to complete the transaction.
Example: withdraw USDT on Ethereum
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-3 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-3 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_table]:my-4 [&_ul]:my-3 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 svelte-wjm2xv" data-component-part="details-disclosure-content">
When you withdraw
USDT
on
Ethereum
, Polyester pays the Ethereum gas fee in
ETH
and charges you the displayed withdrawal fee in USDT. This lets you withdraw USDT without also keeping ETH in your Polyester balance.
Fees can change as network gas costs and asset prices change. Always use the current fee shown for the exact asset and chain you select. A
deposit fee
is deducted from the amount credited, while a
withdrawal fee
is included in the final withdrawal review.
The fee charged by Polyester is separate from any
gas fee
your external wallet charges when you send a deposit to Polyester.
Current fees
Search by token or chain to compare network fees, minimum amounts, and estimated confirmation time.

FAQ
What are the deposit, withdrawal, and transfer fees on Polyester?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
Deposit and withdrawal fees depend on the asset and network. They cover network gas costs plus a small service fee and are charged in the asset you move. Any network fee you pay to send a deposit from an external wallet is separate. Internal transfers within Polyester are free. Polyester sponsors the gas for transactions on
Polyester Chain
Supported Assets
table in the user docs for current deposit and withdrawal fees.
Why do fees or minimums differ between networks?
p]:my-0 [&_ol]:my-2 [&_ol]:list-decimal [&_ol]:pl-6 [&_p]:my-2 [&_p:first-child]:mt-0 [&_p:last-child]:mb-0 [&_strong]:font-medium [&_strong]:text-foreground [&_ul]:my-2 [&_ul]:list-disc [&_ul]:pl-6 w-full">
Each network has its own transaction costs and transfer requirements. Polyester sets fees and minimums for each asset and network, and fees can change as gas costs and asset prices change. See
Supported Assets
in the user docs for the current fees and minimums for each network.
