Asset Flows
Asset Flows
Copy page
View as Markdown
Open in
An
Asset Flow
connects every stage of one deposit, withdrawal, or internal transfer. Instead of making you follow several separate records,
Polyester Scan
brings the movement, status, amounts, and linked transactions into one journey.
The three Flow types
Flow
What it follows
Deposit
An asset moving from an external chain into your Polyester account
Withdrawal
An asset moving from Polyester to an address on a supported external chain
Internal transfer
An asset moving between supported Polyester accounts or balance areas
A Flow is not one blockchain transaction. It can link an external transaction, one or more
Polyester Chain
transactions, validator activity, and a private ledger settlement into one understandable record.
Find a Flow
Open the
Flows directory
to browse recent activity. You can filter by Flow type, account, chain, transaction, asset, or status.
Each row shows the direction, asset, linked records, elapsed time, current stage, and overall status. Open a row to see its complete Summary and Journey.
Read the Summary and Journey
The
Summary
shows what moved and where it went. It includes the asset, principal amount, fee, final amount, source, destination, account, and available transaction links.
The
Journey
shows how the movement progressed. Depending on the Flow, it can include:
source-chain confirmations
Zipper
validator approval
a
Zipped Asset
mint or burn
a Funding or Trading balance update
destination-chain delivery.
Deposit and
withdrawal Flows
contain more stages because they cross networks. An internal transfer usually contains only the observed transfer and its ledger settlement.
Deposit
Withdrawal
Internal transfer
Understand the status
Status
Meaning
Pending
The Flow is still moving through its required stages
Completed
The expected final stage has finished
Failed or Dropped
Processing ended without the intended completion
Refunded
The Flow ended through its supported refund path
The progress bar shows how far the Flow has moved through its lifecycle. A terminal Flow can show 100% whether it completed, failed, or refunded, so the written status remains the clearest result.

A Flow can contain different kinds of records

A linked hexadecimal hash usually opens a blockchain transaction. A ledger settlement reference records a Polyester balance step and is clearly labeled as not being a Polyester Chain transaction.
Follow the linked records
Transaction links take you to the public record for that stage. External-chain links open the relevant network explorer, while Polyester Chain links remain in Polyester Scan.
You can also open the related account, Zipped Asset,
Unified Asset
, or source chain directly from the Flow. This makes the Flow the easiest starting point when you want to understand the complete journey of one asset movement.
