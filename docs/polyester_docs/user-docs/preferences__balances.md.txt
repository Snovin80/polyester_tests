# Balances
Hide account amounts on screen without changing the balances themselves.
Balance settings control whether Polyester shows account amounts on screen. Hiding them does not change the balances themselves.
Open **[Personalization](https://testnet.polyester.com/account/settings/personalization)** from the gear in the top nav to make changes.
## Hide account balances
Turn **Hide Balances** on before sharing your screen or working in a public place. Amounts appear as asterisks until you turn it off.
