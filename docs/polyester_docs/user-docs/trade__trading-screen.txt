The Polyester Trading Screen brings live market data, order entry, private account activity, and balances into one customizable workspace. Choose your chart, personalize its appearance, and arrange every panel around the way you trade.
Open the
Trading Screen
to choose a market and arrange the workspace around the information you use most.
Trading Screen USDT/BTC pair
Public market data and private account data
The Trading Screen keeps public market information and private account activity together without combining their visibility.
Screen area
Visibility
What it shows
Charts, Depth Chart, Order Book, and Market Trades
Public market data
Price history, aggregated liquidity, and completed market-level trades
Open Orders, Order History, Triggers, and Funds
Private account data
Your attributed orders, fills, balances, reservations, and history
The Order Form connects both contexts. It uses the selected Pair's market rules and the active account's available balance and permissions.
Market Trades is not your personal trade history. It shows completed public executions without identifying the accounts behind them. Use Order History and fill details for activity attributed to your account.
