client.triggers
is the standalone automation surface: create a trigger, and when its condition
fires it places a child order for you. Every method is authenticated and account-scoped, so each
input accepts an optional
account
field (
"main"
,
"active"
, or
). See
Account scoping
for how the default resolves.
Triggers come in five types, selected by
triggerType
stop_loss
,
take_profit
,
trailing_stop
,
twap
, and
ladder
execution. Prices and distances are decimal strings, validated against the pair's scale before
the request leaves your process. A decimal above the pair's protobuf wire-format ceiling throws
CatalogConversionError
before network I/O. The ceiling is not an exchange limit.
Unlike the take-profit, stop-loss, and trailing legs you can attach to an order through
client.orders
, a trigger created here has no parent order:
it stands on its own and fires against live market data.

// Stop-loss: submit a market IOC sell when the condition fires

	triggerType: "stop_loss",
	symbolId,
	side: "sell",
	qty: "0.25",
	triggerPrice: "60000",

});

console.log(result.triggerId, result.clientTriggerId, result.acceptedAt);
