Open in
is the official TypeScript client for Polyester.
It wraps the public ConnectRPC API with full types. Requests are validated before they leave your
process. Responses come back as plain TypeScript objects. Prices and quantities are decimal
strings, not floats and not raw scaled integers.
What you can build
Market data: tickers, candles, order books, public trades, heatmaps, chain analytics
Trading: spot orders with attached TP/SL/trailing risk, standalone triggers, fee rates, and trading quotas
Accounts: wallet and

subaccounts

Funding: deposits, ledger transfers, internal transfers, withdrawals
Realtime: typed WebSocket streams with a shared
subscribe(...)
